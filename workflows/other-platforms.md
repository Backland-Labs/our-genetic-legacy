# Other DNA platforms — intake notes

**Owner:** Rosalind. All are PARTICIPANT-ACCOUNT. The participant logs in; DNAversity never asks for credentials. Capability-detect every feature: ask what the participant sees. Last verified: 2026-08-31.

## 23andMe (23-MATCH-01, 23-RIC-02)

- Open **DNA Relatives** and confirm the profile.
- Prefer the participant's downloaded DNA Relatives list if they have one; it includes display names, predicted relationships, and percent shared. Otherwise a screenshot of the top of the list.
- Percent shared, not cM, is the default display. Record percent; estimate cM at roughly 74 cM per 1% if needed and say it is an estimate.
- For a target relative, open them and scroll to **Relatives in Common**. Capture the common relatives and any pairwise predicted relationships.
- Never request or ingest health reports. If a screenshot includes health content, ask for one without it.
- 23andMe has changed and restricted DNA Relatives features before; ask what is available now.

## MyHeritage (MH-MATCH-01)

- Open **DNA → DNA Matches** and select the correct kit.
- The match list shows name, percent and cM, number of shared segments, largest segment, and possible relationship. Capture the top of the list.
- **Shared DNA Matches** for a target match works like Shared Matches.
- AutoClusters, the chromosome browser, and shared-segment downloads exist and can be captured, but are not needed for basic grouping. Do not ask for them until a segment question is active.
- Respect the kit manager's privacy settings; shared-segment visibility may be restricted.

## FamilyTreeDNA (FT-MATCH-01)

- Open **Family Finder Matches**. The page supports CSV export; the match-list CSV is welcome. The raw-data download is a different thing and is not requested.
- **In Common With** for a target match works like Shared Matches.
- Y-DNA and mtDNA are separate tests with separate rules; note them in the profile inventory and leave analysis for a later specialist.

## Living DNA (LD-MATCH-01)

- Open the **Relatives / Family Matching** area and confirm the profile.
- Capture match identity and sharing amount from the list. Shared matches and shared-segment tools exist; capture only when needed.

## GEDmatch (GED-REPORT-01)

- Use only if the participant already uses GEDmatch. Never suggest uploading raw DNA to get there.
- One-to-Many results can be pasted or screenshotted. Kit numbers are identifiers for other people; keep them out of shared outputs.

## Any other provider

Ask: match list? shared or in-common matches? cM or percent? segments? clusters? parent-side labels? trees? Record what is available. Do not infer capabilities the participant has not confirmed.

## Cross-platform

When the same person appears on two sites, keep one `M-` entry with both observations. Ask the participant whether two similarly named matches are the same person; do not assume.
