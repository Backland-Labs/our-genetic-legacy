# Sample case — "Renee" (synthetic)

A fully invented family for practice, facilitator training, and the manual test checklist. No real people. Use it to see how DNAversity behaves before running a real case.

## The situation

Renee (born about 1974, Charleston, SC) knows her mother, Loretta, and her maternal grandparents. She does not know her biological father. Her mother has said only that his name was "Ray" and that he was from Beaufort. Renee tested at AncestryDNA. Her maternal first cousin Kim has also tested there.

## How to run it

1. Start a conversation with DNAversity on and type `/start`.
2. Answer Harriet's Foundation questions from `inputs.md` section 1. Say the father is Unknown.
3. When Harriet asks about DNA, paste the match list from section 2 as text (it stands in for a screenshot).
4. When Rosalind asks for shared matches for a target, paste the relevant block from section 3.
5. Supply the records from section 4 when Carter asks, or with `/upload record`.
6. Try `/question` (Who was Renee's biological father?), `/matches`, `/groups`, `/unknown father`, `/verify`, and `/challenge`.

## What should happen

- Harriet prints the version and privacy notice and asks for two skill levels before anything else.
- The father stays **Unknown** in the People table throughout. Candidates appear only as hypotheses.
- Kim (812 cM, documented maternal 1C, range 396–1397) is promoted to an **Anchor** on the maternal side.
- J.W. (1,102 cM) is *not* called a half-sibling. Rosalind lists the relationships the range admits: grandparent, first cousin, half aunt or uncle, great-aunt or uncle, great-grandparent. Half sibling (1160–2436) is excluded.
- J.W., bmoore72, and T. Simmons form **Group A**, provisional at first, strengthened when they co-occur. Group A is not named "Simmons" until reconstructed pedigrees converge.
- Delores P. shares with Kim and is placed as a maternal-side candidate, not confirmed.
- gwen_h shares with no one captured yet and stays ungrouped.
- The 1950 census "granddaughter" entry for Loretta is recorded as a **conflict**, not resolved by preference.
- Pauli refuses Confirmed for any father candidate and names the exact gap.
- Ida returns at least one alternative (for example, J.W. as a half aunt through a sister of the father, or a maternal-side explanation via an unrecorded relationship) and the evidence that would distinguish it.
- No one is asked for a password, a raw DNA file, or to contact J.W.
