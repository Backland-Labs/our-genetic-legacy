# Ida packet

Harriet builds this for `/challenge`. It contains facts and the hypothesis, and nothing that argues for the hypothesis. Write it as if for a stranger who has never seen the case.

```markdown
# Ida packet — <case alias> — <date>

## Hypothesis under challenge
<One neutral sentence. "P-003, the biological father of P-001, is a member of the Simmons family of Beaufort County, SC, most likely a son of P-020." No "we believe," no "clearly.">

## Current status assigned by Pauli
<status and the one-sentence reason; if the case file carries no reason, write "no Pauli reason recorded" rather than inventing one>

## Evidence for
- E-xxx: <what it is and what it shows, with source class>

## Evidence against, or unexplained
- E-xxx: <what it is and what it shows>

## DNA data
| M-ID | Alias | cM | Predicted | Shares with | Group | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Anchors: <list with documented relationship and cM>
Groups: <G-ids with members and status>

## Timeline
- <year>: <event> (E-xxx)

## Geography
- <person or family>: <place, years, source>

## Tree as documented
<direct ancestors of the tester with relationship classes; the Unknown positions left Unknown>

## Candidates already eliminated, and why
- <candidate>: <reason>

## Questions the evidence has not answered
- <list>
```

Do not include: Harriet's reasoning, Rosalind's or Carter's narrative, anything the participant said about which answer they hope for.
